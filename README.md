# CreativeCodingA3

Study Song #1 is a calm and repetitive song, designed to minimise distractions while studying. 
Study Song #2 is a fast-paced and energetic song, designed for motivated students. 
Study Song #3 gradually builds in intensity to help tired or overwhelmed students regain focus.
By simply opening each file, the study song plays. 
The .json files for each song show the lines of code, as the mp3 does not.
